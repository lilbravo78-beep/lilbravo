# Changes from v2 to v3

1. **More space for the lecture slide.** The Live page now allocates approximately 73% of the side-by-side content area to English slide material and 27% to short Chinese support notes at desktop widths. On small screens, the panels stack vertically.
2. **Students choose their own vocabulary.** Select a word or phrase directly in the English slide and click **+ Add a word**. A small editor allows changing the term, adding a Chinese meaning, and entering a personal note. Any word can also be typed manually.
3. **Words appear in Review.** Each saved word creates its own Review card with the originating slide, optional translation and note, plus reveal/understood/remove controls. The record persists in this browser. Previously implemented slide-concept and caption-question cards still work.
4. **No new AI claims.** The English slide content and Chinese notes are still prepared examples. The new word-saving function is implemented in the browser; no automatic PPT parsing or live translation has been added.

For the A2 report, this is a new interaction flow that should be reflected in the storyboard and the system diagram.
