v4 · offline interactive demo
Prototype scope: The page navigation, glossary import, student-selected vocabulary, and separate vocabulary/caption review lists work locally. Lecture speech, translated captions, slide explanations and AI alerts are prepared demonstrations. This is not a live speech translation or automatic PPT-analysis system.
